# Evaluation files

Zero-shot, rotation, bootstrap and ablation evaluations of the trained models of `results/` (seed 42 unless stated).
Every file is written by a script of `scripts/`, named in the second column; the trained runs they evaluate are the
directories of `results/<task>/`.  Metrics are SSIM, Pearson, NRMSE and R2 on the first 100 test samples (`test100`)
unless the key says otherwise; `stored_test100` / `recomputed_test100` in several files record that the evaluation
first reproduces the `test100` metrics of `result.json`.

| file | written by | contents |
|---|---|---|
| `ns_zero_shot.json` | `scripts/eval_frozen_units.py` | the NS vorticity model (`results/ns_vorticity/rhmp_s42`) on the training grid `G1`, a second stretched grid `G2`, uniform grids with 32^2 and 64^2 nodes (`U32`, `U64`) and the training grid rotated by 90 degrees (`G1rot`); each on the 1500 validation samples (`val`) and on `test100`; modes `default` (per-mesh normalisers), `frozen_units` (normalisers of the training mesh) and `frozen_auto` (the per-column rule: training units while a mesh's descriptor median lies in the training range; `frozen_auto_columns` lists the decision of every column on every grid); `result_json` repeats the `test100` and `zs_*` entries of `result.json` |
| `torus_zero_shot.json` | `scripts/eval_torus_aniso.py` | the torus model (`results/torus_transport/rhmp_s42`): `test100` (reproduction of `result.json`), `sets`: the 450 test samples (`test450`), the toroidal speed raised beyond the training range (`alpha2`, `alpha2.5`), anisotropy ratios 300 and 1000 (`ratio300`, `ratio1000`), two applications of the model against the solution at twice the horizon (`rollout`), the test instances on the 2x finer / coarser tori (`fine`, `coarse`; `_t100`: the 100 paper samples only), the validation instances on the training / fine / coarse tori (`val_*`), rigid rotations of the torus by 30, 90, 137 degrees (`rot*`; `rot0` / `rot360` are identity checks); `mass`: relative mass change of prediction and target |
| `torus_zero_shot_frozen_auto.json` | `scripts/eval_torus_aniso.py --frozen-auto` | the mesh-transfer sets of the torus model again with `frozen_descriptors='auto'` (`frozen_units_auto`: metrics, the per-column decisions on the three tori, and the check that the training-mesh output is unchanged) |
| `wilson_loop_zero_shot.json`, `yang_mills_zero_shot.json` | `scripts/transfer_eval/run_eval.py` (test data: `scripts/transfer_eval/gen_planar.py`) | the gauge-field models (`results/wilson_loop/rhmp_s42`, `results/yang_mills/rhmp_s42`) on the original test mesh (`orig`, the stored data; `orig_regen`, the same mesh with regenerated fields), a new random Delaunay mesh with 1024 nodes (`new`), its 3:1 anisotropic re-triangulation (`aniso`), a 4096-node mesh (`fine`), three further mesh realisations (`new_s<k>`, `aniso_s<k>`) and a graded mesh (`graded`); for the U(1) model also gauge transformations `theta -> theta + d0 lambda` with `lambda` of 0.1, 1 and 10 times the RMS of `theta` (`gauge_*`); modes `default`, `frozen_units`, `frozen_auto`; `condition_checks` verifies every regenerated data set (`d1 theta` against the stored face cochain, orientation, cell counts) |
| `wilson_loop_rotations.json`, `yang_mills_rotations.json` | `scripts/frame_eval/run_eval.py` | the same two models on the test mesh rotated rigidly about its centroid by 0, 30, 90, 137 and 360 degrees (`angles`), with the three normaliser modes |
| `maxwell_runs.json` | `scripts/maxwell_tables.py` (after `scripts/maxwell_eval.py`) | every run of the magnetostatics study (`runs/maxwell/MXA/...` of `scripts/run_maxwell.sh`): the paper model `solver_T_J4_nogeo` (seeds 42, 1, 2), its variants (`solver_T_J2*`, `solver_T_J0_nogeo`, `solver_D_J2_nogeo`, `solver_T_J2_noorient`, `solver_T_frozen`, `stack_T_J2`, `poly4`) and the baselines (seed 42 and the seeds present when the file was written; the complete seed-1 / seed-2 baselines are in `results/maxwell_magnetostatics/`); per run `test100`, `test`, `zero_shot` (`ood15`: currents x 1.5, `heldout`: the E-E inductor-core family absent from training, `fine` / `coarse`: 7255- / 459-node meshes; `*_test100` on the 100 paper samples) and `rotation` (metrics after rigid rotations of the device by 30, 90, 137 degrees; seed-42 runs) |
| `airfrans_pressure_runs.json` | `scripts/airfrans_pressure_collect.py` (after `scripts/airfrans_pressure_eval.py`) | every run of the AirfRANS pressure study (`runs/airfrans/<task>/...` of `scripts/run_pressure.sh`): the baselines on `AFP` (seeds 42, 1, 2), the paper model `rhmp_s*` on `AFPi0`, its leave-one-out ablations (`abl_*`: no projection layer, no energy readout, no inflow 1-form, no dynamic similarity, no Kutta correction, bounded radial gate), variants (`var_*`: angle-of-attack scalar, log U input, pressure-coefficient target) and the baseline controls (`AFPcp`, `AFPxy`, `AFPcpxy`, `AFPpf`: pressure-coefficient target, chord-frame positions, both, potential-flow pressure as an input); per run `test100` (physical units), `zero_shot` (`fine` / `coarse`: the same simulations on 6.9K- / 0.55K-node meshes; `wall_R2` on the airfoil surface), `rotation` (R2 after rigid rotations of the configuration by 30, 90, 137 degrees), `wall` (metrics on the airfoil surface) and `lift` (mean absolute error and R2 of the lift coefficient) |
| `bootstrap_test100_seed42.json` | test-set bootstrap of the seed-42 runs | for every task (`T1` NS vorticity, `T2` torus, `T3` ellipsoid, `T5` magnetostatics, `T6` Wilson loop, `T7` Yang-Mills, `T8` AirfRANS) and model: mean and standard deviation of SSIM, Pearson and NRMSE over 1000 resamples of the 100 test samples (the bootstrap appendix of the paper) |
| `table_metrics_seed42.json`, `table_metrics_seeds.json` | collected from the `result.json` files | the `test100` metrics, parameter counts and best validation R2 of the table runs, keyed by task code (`T1` ... `T8` as above), model and seed (seed 42 only / seeds 42, 1, 2); the same numbers as `results/<task>/<model>_s<seed>/result.json`, in one file |

`scripts/collect_results.py` regenerates `results/RESULTS.md` from the run directories and reads
`airfrans_pressure_runs.json` for the zero-shot columns of the AirfRANS table.
